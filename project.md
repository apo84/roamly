Project Name (Working Title)

Roamly

1. Executive Summary

This project is a geo-spatial discovery platform that converts unstructured short-form social media travel videos into structured, map-based, actionable itineraries.

Independent travelers currently suffer from planning fatigue due to fragmented inspiration sources (TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, etc.) and the manual effort required to extract usable location data from videos. Our product eliminates multi-platform cross-validation by extracting, geo-tagging, and organizing high-signal video content into a searchable map interface.

This is a B2C subscription/freemium product targeting time-constrained independent travelers, especially young professionals with limited PTO and high disposable income.

2. Problem Definition

Independent travelers struggle with planning fatigue when trying to find authentic local destinations because relevant information is trapped in unorganized, non-searchable social media videos.

We narrowed scope to:

Extracting geo-data from short-form video. Structuring it into searchable, mappable outputs. This shift focuses on the highest-leverage friction in the workflow.

3. Users & Stakeholders

Independent traveler.

Characteristics: Planning international or domestic trips. Seeks authentic “hidden gems." Uses TikTok and Instagram for inspiration. Feels overwhelmed by fragmented research. Values efficiency over deep manual curation. They may also upload their own content post-trip.


4. Value Proposition

We help independent travelers achieve a 50% reduction in research time and eliminate planning fatigue by aggregating and geo-tagging high-signal social media travel videos into a single searchable interface compared to manual multi-platform cross-validation across TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, and notes apps.

Important:

Value is measured in time saved and stress reduced. Alternative is not another travel app — it is the current manual workflow.



6. MVP Definition

The MVP is the smallest product that allows a user to convert a curated feed of geo-tagged TikTok and Instagram travel videos into a functional map-based itinerary and experience the elimination of multi-platform manual cross-validation.

If users can:

Import content, See accurate map pins, Build an itinerary then the MVP succeeds.

6.2 Explicit Non-Goals (Out of Scope for MVP)

We are NOT building:

Direct booking engine (flights, hotels, reservations).

In-app video hosting or storage backend.

Social networking features (friends lists, DMs, follower feeds).

Fully AI-generated trip itineraries.

Offline map mode.

Flight price comparison.

Hotel recommendation engine.

Rewards/points system.

Creator monetization marketplace.

Full social media replacement platform.

This cycle is focused purely on:
Parsing → Structuring → Mapping → Itinerary Conversion.

7. Core User Journey (Happy Path)

Discovery
User sees ad or shared link while feeling planning fatigue for upcoming trip (e.g., Barcelona).

Onboarding
Sign in with Google or Apple. Select destination and travel dates.

Content Sync
Prompt: “Sync Instagram/TikTok Saves” or select curated city feed.

Processing State
System extracts geo-data. User sees “Processing Locations” animation.

Interactive Map
Video thumbnails pinned to exact physical locations.

Aha Moment
User clicks a pin.
Watches 15-second vibe clip.
Sees “Add to Itinerary” + walking distance from hotel.

Trust gap closes:
Inspiration → Logistics in one place.

Itinerary Export
User exports route to Google Maps.

Repeat Use Mechanism
“Digital Passport” check-ins create a shareable map of visited hidden gems.

Stickiness driver:
Utility + identity + social proof.

9. Positioning Statement

For time-constrained independent travelers who experience planning fatigue, our product is a geo-social discovery engine that transforms social media travel videos into map-based, actionable itineraries. Unlike Google Maps or TikTok alone, we convert unstructured video inspiration into searchable, location-accurate trip plans in minutes.
